# GynCell Compass

GynCell Compass is a free, interactive tool that helps researchers identify cervical, ovarian and uterine cell lines suited to their research questions. It was developed alongside the review “Which Cell Line Is the Right One? – Rethinking Model Selection in Gynaecological Cancer Research” (currently under review).

Choose a cancer type, mark relevant characteristics as Required or Preferred, and reorder categories to reflect your priorities. Expand cell-line cards to explore detailed characteristics and sources, view your selection as a decision tree, and export the tree as PDF or JPEG. Candidate results can be downloaded as CSV, and the complete Excel master file is available within the tool.

Use GynCell Compass as a starting point and critically assess each cell line’s suitability for your experiment. Please contact the study’s authors with questions, corrections or suggestions for future updates.

Current version: 11/2026.
